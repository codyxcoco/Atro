# Atro Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Atro is a local-first workout journal for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how Atro handles information when you use the app.

## Summary

- Atro does not sell your data.
- Atro does not use third-party advertising SDKs.
- Atro does not use third-party analytics SDKs.
- Atro does not require an account.
- Data you enter in Atro is stored locally on your device unless you choose to send something out yourself, such as syncing to Calendar or exporting a file.

## Information Atro Stores

Atro stores the following information locally on your device so the app can work:

- workout templates
- planned workouts
- logged workouts
- workout notes and tags
- State of Body entries
- meal entries
- app settings

This information is stored on-device and is not transmitted to Atro-operated servers by the app.

## Health Data

If you enable Apple Health integration, Atro may:

- read workouts
- read active energy
- read exercise minutes
- write completed workouts that you explicitly save, if you turn on workout export

Health data is used only to power Atro features such as Today context, Journal matching, and optional workout export. Atro does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage purposes.

If you do not enable Health access, Atro continues to work without Health data.

## Calendar Data

If you enable Calendar sync, Atro may add planned workouts to your Calendar using write-only Calendar access. These events can include:

- workout title
- scheduled date and time
- planned duration
- workout notes
- a deep link back into Atro

Atro does not need full read access to your Calendar for this feature.

If you do not enable Calendar access, Atro can still export an `.ics` file when you choose to do so.

## User-Initiated Sharing and Export

Atro may let you export or share content, such as a Calendar `.ics` file, when you explicitly choose to do so. Once you send information to another app, service, or destination, that destination's privacy practices will apply.

## Data Collection

Atro is designed so that data entered into the app stays on your device by default. Atro does not collect personal information from the app and does not send your workout, meal, journal, or Health data to Atro-operated servers.

If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that version is released.

## Tracking, Analytics, and Advertising

Atro does not use tracking for advertising purposes.

Atro does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

Atro does not include third-party analytics SDKs.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains on your device until you delete it.

You can remove your local Atro data at any time from within the app using:

`Settings` > `Delete All Data`

You can also revoke Calendar and Health permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## Children

Atro is not directed to children under the age of 13.
